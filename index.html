Frequently asked questions

How long does an order take to arrive?

On average 25 to 40 minutes after the order is confirmed, depending on your delivery area and how long the dishes take to cook. The final estimate appears on your receipt after you order.

Is there a minimum order?

There is no minimum order. Free delivery applies to subtotals from Rp75.000, and some vouchers have their own minimum spend.

How can I pay?

QRIS, GoPay, OVO, Dana, and cash to the courier are available. Choose a method in your cart before pressing the order button.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, as long as the restaurant has not started cooking. Open Order history in your account, then tap Cancel on an order that is still awaiting confirmation.

My order is wrong or late. What should I do?

Contact us through the form in the Contact section and mention your receipt number. We will look into the order.

Is my account data safe?

This is a demo site. Accounts, orders, and carts are stored only in this browser and are never sent to any server. Do not use your real password.
